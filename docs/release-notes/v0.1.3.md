## Added

- Control remote access in Settings with the new **Access over Tailscale** switch. Turning it off keeps Remote available locally on your Mac.

## Fixed

- The app respects `EXPOSE=off` at startup and preserves your access preference across restarts.
- Turning access off removes only Remote's route, preserving other services on Tailscale.
- Disabled access and setup errors are reflected in the menu and phone-link window, with a retry action when setup fails.
